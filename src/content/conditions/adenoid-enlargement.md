intro: >-
  The adenoids are small pads of tissue at the back of the nose that form part
  of the immune system. In some children they become enlarged, which can block
  the nasal airway and lead to mouth breathing, snoring and disturbed sleep.
symptoms:
  - Persistent blocked nose and mouth breathing
  - Snoring or noisy breathing during sleep
  - Restless or disturbed sleep
  - Nasal-sounding speech
  - Frequent colds or ear and sinus problems
triggers:
  - Ongoing nasal allergy and inflammation
  - Recurrent upper respiratory infections
whoAffected: >-
  Adenoid enlargement is most common in children. It is often seen together
  with allergic rhinitis and recurrent respiratory infections.
whenToSeekHelp: >-
  Seek advice if your child has ongoing mouth breathing, loud snoring,
  noticeably disturbed sleep, or recurrent nose, throat and ear problems.
consultationApproach: >-
  A consultation reviews the child's symptom pattern, sleep, breathing and
  history of infections and allergy, so that care can be discussed and further
  evaluation arranged where appropriate.

  - question: Why is my child always breathing through the mouth?
    answer: >-
      Persistent mouth breathing can be caused by nasal blockage from enlarged
      adenoids, allergy or both. An assessment can help identify the underlying
      reason.

Enlarged adenoids can affect a child's breathing, sleep and general wellbeing.
Because symptoms often overlap with nasal allergy, understanding the whole
picture helps in planning care.

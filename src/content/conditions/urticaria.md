intro: >-
  Urticaria, commonly called hives, appears as raised, itchy welts on the skin.
  They can come and go, vary in size, and sometimes appear without an obvious
  cause. For some people they are occasional; for others they recur over weeks
  or longer.
symptoms:
  - Raised, itchy welts or wheals on the skin
  - Redness around the affected areas
  - Welts that change shape, move or come and go
  - Itching that can disturb sleep and comfort
triggers:
  - Certain foods or additives in sensitive individuals
  - Insect bites and stings
  - Heat, cold, pressure or sunlight in some people
  - Infections and, at times, no identifiable trigger
whoAffected: >-
  Urticaria can affect people of any age. It may occur once, occasionally, or
  in a recurring pattern.
whenToSeekHelp: >-
  Seek advice if hives keep returning, last a long time, or affect your sleep
  and daily life. Seek urgent care if hives occur with swelling of the lips,
  tongue or throat, or with any difficulty breathing.

Hives can be unpredictable and uncomfortable. Understanding when they appear and
what might set them off is an important step in managing recurring episodes.

> If hives are accompanied by swelling of the face, lips or throat, or any
> difficulty breathing, seek emergency care immediately.

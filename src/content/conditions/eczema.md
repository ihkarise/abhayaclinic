summary: Dry, itchy, inflamed skin that can flare and settle over time, often linked to allergy and sensitive skin.
intro: >-
  Eczema (atopic dermatitis) is a condition in which the skin becomes dry,
  itchy and inflamed. It often follows a pattern of flare-ups and calmer
  periods, and is commonly associated with a personal or family history of
  allergy.
symptoms:
  - Dry, rough or scaly skin
  - Persistent itching, sometimes intense
  - Redness and inflammation
  - Skin that may crack, weep or thicken over time
  - Discomfort affecting sleep and daily activity
triggers:
  - Soaps, detergents and other irritants
  - Dust, pollen and other allergens
  - Heat, sweating and dry weather
  - Stress and, in some people, certain foods
whoAffected: >-
  Eczema often begins in childhood but can continue into or start in adulthood.
  It is frequently seen alongside allergic rhinitis and asthma.
whenToSeekHelp: >-
  Consider a consultation if itching disturbs sleep, the skin is frequently
  inflamed or infected, or flare-ups are affecting comfort and confidence.
consultationApproach: >-
  A consultation looks at your skin's pattern of flare-ups, likely triggers,
  daily routine and history, so that care and skin-care guidance can be tailored
  to your situation.
faq:
  - question: Is eczema contagious?
    answer: >-
      No. Eczema is not contagious. It relates to skin sensitivity and
      inflammation rather than infection, although broken skin can sometimes
      become infected.

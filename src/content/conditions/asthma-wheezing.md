intro: >-
  Asthma and wheezing involve narrowing and inflammation of the airways, which
  can make breathing difficult. For many people symptoms are closely linked to
  allergic triggers, exercise, infections or changes in the weather.
symptoms:
  - Recurrent cough, often worse at night or early morning
  - Wheezing or a whistling sound when breathing
  - Chest tightness or discomfort
  - Shortness of breath
  - Symptoms that flare with triggers or infections
triggers:
  - Dust, pollen, pet dander and other airborne allergens
  - Respiratory infections
  - Cold air, smoke and strong smells
  - Physical exertion in sensitive individuals
whoAffected: >-
  Asthma often begins in childhood but can appear at any age. It is frequently
  associated with other allergic conditions such as allergic rhinitis and
  eczema, and may run in families.
whenToSeekHelp: >-
  Breathing problems should always be taken seriously. Seek prompt medical care
  for severe breathlessness, and arrange a consultation if cough, wheeze or
  chest tightness keep recurring or disturb sleep and activity.
consultationApproach: >-
  A consultation reviews your symptom pattern, triggers and history to
  understand how the condition affects you. Ongoing breathing conditions may
  need appropriate monitoring and coordination of care.
faq:
  - question: Is wheezing always asthma?
    answer: >-
      Not necessarily. Wheezing can have several causes, which is why recurring
      symptoms should be assessed properly rather than assumed.

Recurring cough and wheeze can significantly affect sleep, activity and quality
of life. Because breathing symptoms can have several causes, an assessment
helps clarify the pattern and how care should be approached.

> Severe or sudden breathing difficulty is a medical emergency — seek immediate
> care rather than waiting for a routine consultation.
